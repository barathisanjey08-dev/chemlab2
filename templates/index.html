⌘ SMILES

SMILES is a text notation for representing molecular structures. Atoms, bonds, branches, and rings are encoded as characters.

CCO → ethanol
◇ Molecular weight

Calculated from the atomic masses of the atoms in a molecule, commonly expressed in g/mol.

↔ LogP

An estimate of partitioning between a nonpolar phase and water; a model descriptor of hydrophobicity.

▦ TPSA

Topological polar surface area estimates contributions from polar atoms and can support molecular comparisons.

⋈ H-bonding

Donor and acceptor counts are rule-based structural descriptors, not complete predictions of biological behavior.

✧ 3D conformers

A computed conformer is a modelled shape, not necessarily the only shape a flexible molecule can adopt.
